Who I am

    

      

        
I'm a senior software developer working mostly on backend systems and the infrastructure underneath them — services that need to stay fast and correct as traffic grows, not just work on my laptop. My background spans fintech, e-commerce and developer tools, and I've spent most of that time on teams shipping to production continuously rather than in long release cycles.

        
Outside of the day job, I write about the engineering decisions that don't make it into the changelog, contribute to a couple of open-source projects, and mentor junior engineers who are past tutorials and into the messy part of the job.

      

      

        

          
Keep it simple

          
Prefer solutions that are easy to understand, maintain, and hand off — cleverness is a cost, not a feature.

        

        

          
Build for scale

          
Design with reliability, performance, and future growth in mind, without over-engineering for load that may never come.

        

        

          
Automate everything

          
If a human does it twice, it becomes a script. Repetitive work is where bugs and burnout both live.

        

        

          
Measure before optimizing

          
Use data and observability to find the real bottleneck before spending a sprint chasing the imagined one.

        

        

          
Write code for humans

          
Code is read far more often than it's written. Readable beats clever, every time it matters.
